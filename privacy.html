Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What we collect

Email address and password (hashed) for your account; your coin balance, completed offers, referrals and withdrawal requests; payout details you provide (PayPal email or crypto address); device and IP signals used only for fraud prevention; and cookies (see below). We do not collect your survey answers — those go to the survey provider, not to us.

How we use it

To run your account, credit coins, pay you, prevent fraud, and answer support requests. We never sell your personal data. We share the minimum necessary with service providers who run the site for us (hosting, database, offerwall networks, payment processors) under their own privacy terms.

Cookies & ads

We use cookies for login sessions, fraud prevention, and advertising. Third-party ad networks on this site may use cookies to serve ads and measure them; you can opt out of personalized ads in your browser settings or via the network's opt-out page. We never pay users to view or click ads.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correction, or deletion of your account at any time by contacting support. Deleting your account forfeits any unpaid coin balance. US state privacy laws may give you additional rights (access, deletion, opt-out of sale/sharing); contact us to exercise them.

Children

SurveyCoins is for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us.
