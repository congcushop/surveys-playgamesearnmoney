When do I get paid?

Request a withdrawal at $10 (PayPal) or $3 (crypto). First withdrawals are manually reviewed within 24–72 hours; payouts are processed in batches on Tuesdays and Fridays.

Why do some surveys disqualify me?

Advertisers want specific people (age, location, habits). About 1 in 5 surveys screen out — that's normal on every survey site. Try another one; new surveys arrive all day.
