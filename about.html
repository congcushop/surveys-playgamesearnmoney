About SurveyCoins

SurveyCoins is a rewards website: you complete surveys, play games and try apps, and advertisers pay us for it. We share 60% of every offer's value with you as coins — 1,000 coins = $1 — and you cash out from $10 via PayPal or $3 in crypto.

We started with one belief: reward sites should be honest. No "earn $500 in a day" claims, no fake payout screenshots, no charging you to withdraw. Earnings depend on what you do — surveys pay $0.30–$3, games $3–$12 (up to $50+ for big milestones), trials $1–$4. Our payout proof wall only ever shows real, paid withdrawals.

SurveyCoins is the surveys-led site of the PlayGamesEarnMoney family. Our sister sites cover game offers and free trials — same account system, same coins, same payouts.
